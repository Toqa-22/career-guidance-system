Used for the buttons and accents on this exam's own public page.

When off (the default), this exam has no time limit — a participant answers at their own pace and submits manually, with no countdown shown. When on, the Duration below starts a countdown the moment a participant opens the exam, and it auto-submits whatever was answered so far the instant it reaches zero.

Enter it as minutes.seconds — the digits after the dot ARE the seconds, not a fraction of a minute. So 2.30 means 2 min 30 sec, and 2.5 means 2 min 5 sec (not 2 min 30 sec). Just 30 (no dot) means 30 min.
